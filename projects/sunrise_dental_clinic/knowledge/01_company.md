# Company — Sunrise Dental Clinic

## Company Overview

### Company Name

Sunrise Dental Clinic

---

### Tagline

Healthy Smiles, Every Sunrise.

---

### Business Description

Sunrise Dental Clinic is a family-owned dental practice providing general, cosmetic, and emergency dental care. The practice focuses on making dental visits comfortable and stress-free for patients of every age, from young children to seniors.

---

### Mission

To provide gentle, modern dental care that helps every patient feel confident about their smile and comfortable in the chair.

---

### Vision

To be the most trusted dental practice in the Portland metro area, known for compassionate care, modern technology, and making patients feel like family.

---

### Core Values

- Compassion — every patient is treated with patience and warmth
- Comfort — reducing dental anxiety is part of the job, not an afterthought
- Integrity — honest recommendations, never unnecessary treatment
- Excellence — modern techniques and continuing education for every provider
- Community — active involvement in local health and school programs

## Target Audience

### Primary Customers

Local families and individuals seeking routine and preventive dental care.

---

### Secondary Customers

Patients seeking cosmetic dentistry (whitening, veneers) and adults requiring restorative work (implants, crowns).

---

### Ideal Customer Profile

Portland-metro residents who want a consistent, long-term dental home for their whole family, including patients who have historically avoided the dentist due to anxiety.

---

### Customer Needs

- Convenient scheduling (including after-work and Saturday hours)
- Clear explanation of costs and insurance coverage before treatment
- A calm, judgment-free environment for anxious patients
- Pediatric-friendly care for parents booking for their children

---

### Customer Pain Points

- Difficulty getting a timely appointment for dental emergencies
- Confusion about what insurance does and doesn't cover
- Dental anxiety that has caused delayed or avoided care
- Not knowing whether a cosmetic procedure is right for them

---

## Competitive Position

### Unique Value Proposition

A modern dental practice that treats anxious and first-time patients with the same warmth as long-time regulars — without sacrificing the latest dental technology.

---

### Competitive Advantages

- Same-week emergency appointments
- Digital X-rays (lower radiation, instant results)
- Saturday hours, rare among local practices
- Bilingual front-office and clinical staff

---

### Key Differentiators

- Focus on comfort-first care for anxious patients
- Family-oriented scheduling (whole family seen in one visit when possible)
- Transparent, upfront pricing before any procedure
