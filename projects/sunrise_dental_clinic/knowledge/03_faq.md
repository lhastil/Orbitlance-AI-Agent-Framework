## 1. Do you accept my dental insurance?

**Category:** Billing | **Priority:** High

**Answer:** We accept most major dental insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, and MetLife. Bring your insurance card to your first visit and our front desk will verify your coverage before treatment.

**Alternative phrasings:** "Is my insurance accepted?", "Do you take [insurance name]?"

**Escalation:** Conditional — the AI can confirm general insurance acceptance, but must transfer to front-desk staff for coverage verification specific to a patient's plan.

## 2. What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

**Category:** Services | **Priority:** High

**Answer:** Call us right away — we hold same-week emergency slots every day. If it's after hours, our system will let you know the next available emergency opening. For severe trauma (like a knocked-out tooth), keep the tooth moist and seek care as soon as possible.

**Related Services:** Emergency Dental Care

**Escalation:** No — the AI can provide this guidance directly, but should always encourage the patient to call immediately for anything severe.

## 3. Do you see children?

**Category:** Services | **Priority:** Medium

**Answer:** Yes — we welcome patients starting from their first tooth. Our pediatric appointments are designed to be calm and comfortable for young patients, and we can often see siblings or parents back-to-back.

**Related Services:** Pediatric Dentistry

**Escalation:** No.

## 4. How often should I get a cleaning?

**Category:** General | **Priority:** Medium

**Answer:** Most patients should have a cleaning and checkup every six months. Patients with gum disease or other risk factors may be recommended a more frequent schedule by their dentist.

**Escalation:** Conditional — general guidance is fine, but a specific recommendation for an individual patient's dental health belongs to the dentist, not the AI.

---

## 5. Do you offer payment plans?

**Category:** Pricing | **Priority:** Medium

**Answer:** Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for larger treatments like implants or veneers. Our front-desk team can walk you through the options during your consultation.

**Related Processes:** Consultation Process

**Escalation:** Conditional — the AI can confirm payment plans exist, but exact terms should be confirmed by front-desk staff.

**Category:** Pricing | **Priority:** Medium

**Answer:** A standard adult cleaning and exam starts at $120 without insurance. Exact pricing can vary slightly based on X-ray needs — see our Pricing Knowledge for full details.

**Related FAQ:** "Do you accept my dental insurance?"
