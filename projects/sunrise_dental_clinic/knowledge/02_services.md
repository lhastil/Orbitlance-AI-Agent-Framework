## General Checkups & Cleanings

### Category

Preventive Care | Status: Active

### Summary

Routine dental exams, professional cleanings, and cavity screening for patients of all ages.

### Business Value / Problem Solved

Prevents small issues from becoming costly, painful problems and keeps patients on track with insurance-covered preventive care.

### Target Audience

All patients, especially families establishing a regular dental home.

### Key Benefits

- Early detection of cavities and gum disease
- Typically fully or mostly covered by insurance
- Same visit includes exam, cleaning, and X-rays if due

### Typical Use Cases

Twice-yearly checkups, new-patient first visit, school/sports physicals requiring a dental note.

### Expected Outcomes

Healthier teeth and gums, and an ongoing relationship with a consistent dental provider.

### Pricing

See Pricing Knowledge — "Preventive Care" pricing.

### Consultation Available

No — this is a standard bookable appointment, not a consultation.

### Related Services

Pediatric Dentistry, Emergency Dental Care

## Cosmetic Dentistry (Whitening & Veneers)

### Category

Cosmetic | Status: Active

### Summary

Professional teeth whitening and porcelain veneers to improve the appearance of a patient's smile.

### Business Value / Problem Solved

Helps patients feel confident about their smile for events, career needs, or general self-esteem.

### Target Audience

Adults interested in improving the appearance of their smile; not medically necessary treatment.

### Key Benefits

- In-office whitening results in a single visit
- Veneers custom-shaded to match natural teeth
- Long-lasting results with proper care

### Typical Use Cases

Upcoming wedding or event, general desire for a brighter smile, chipped or unevenly shaped front teeth.

### Expected Outcomes

A brighter, more even smile the patient feels confident about.

### Pricing

Whitening has a starting price; veneers require an in-person consultation. See Pricing Knowledge.

### Consultation Available

Yes — required before veneer treatment; whitening can be booked directly.

### Related Services

Dental Implants

## Emergency Dental Care

### Category

Urgent Care | Status: Active

### Summary

Same-week (often same-day) appointments for dental pain, broken teeth, and other urgent issues.

### Business Value / Problem Solved

Solves the common pain point of not being able to get timely help for sudden dental pain or injury.

### Target Audience

Any patient experiencing dental pain, trauma, or a dental emergency, including non-established patients.

### Key Benefits

- Same-week emergency slots held daily
- Clear guidance on what to do while waiting for the appointment
- No existing patient relationship required

### Typical Use Cases

Severe toothache, broken or knocked-out tooth, lost filling or crown, dental abscess.

### Expected Outcomes

Pain relief and a treatment plan to resolve the underlying issue.

### Pricing

Varies by diagnosis — determined at the emergency visit.

### Consultation Available

The emergency visit itself functions as the initial consultation.

### Related Services

General Checkups & Cleanings, Dental Implants

## Pediatric Dentistry

### Category

Family / Preventive Care | Status: Active

### Summary

Dental care tailored to infants, children, and teens in a calm, kid-friendly environment.

### Business Value / Problem Solved

Helps children build positive associations with dental visits early, reducing dental anxiety later in life.

### Target Audience

Parents of children from first tooth eruption through the teen years.

### Key Benefits

- Kid-friendly exam rooms and staff trained in pediatric comfort techniques
- Preventive sealants and fluoride treatments
- Can often be scheduled alongside a parent's own appointment

### Typical Use Cases

First dental visit (typically around age one), routine childhood checkups, school-required dental exams.

### Expected Outcomes

A positive first experience with dental care and healthy dental habits established early.

### Pricing

See Pricing Knowledge — "Preventive Care" pricing (children's cleanings priced the same as adult cleanings).

### Consultation Available

No — standard bookable appointment.

### Related Services

General Checkups & Cleanings

## Dental Implants

### Category

Restorative | Status: Active

### Summary

Permanent tooth replacement for missing or severely damaged teeth.

### Business Value / Problem Solved

Restores full chewing function and appearance for patients missing one or more teeth, as a longer-lasting alternative to bridges or dentures.

### Target Audience

Adults missing one or more teeth due to injury, decay, or extraction.

### Key Benefits

- Functions and looks like a natural tooth
- Doesn't require altering neighboring healthy teeth
- Long-term solution compared to removable dentures

### Typical Use Cases

Single missing tooth, multiple missing teeth, replacing an ill-fitting denture.

### Expected Outcomes

Restored ability to chew comfortably and a natural-looking smile.

### Pricing

Requires an in-person consultation and imaging before any price can be quoted — highly case-dependent.

### Consultation Available

Yes — required before any implant treatment can be scheduled.

### Related Services

Cosmetic Dentistry, Emergency Dental Care
