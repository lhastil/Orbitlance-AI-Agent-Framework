# Pricing — Sunrise Dental Clinic

## Pricing Overview

### Pricing Strategy

Transparent, fee-for-service pricing for routine care; consultation-based custom quotes for major restorative or cosmetic work.

### Pricing Model

Fixed Price (routine care) + Custom Quote (implants, veneers, and other case-dependent treatment)

### Pricing Factors

Insurance coverage, whether X-rays are needed at the visit, and — for restorative/cosmetic work — case complexity determined at a consultation.

### Consultation Policy

Any treatment requiring an in-person exam or imaging (implants, veneers, root canals) requires a paid or insurance-covered consultation before a price can be quoted.

## Additional Costs

### Optional Services

### Setup Fees

None.

## Discounts

### Available Discounts

10% discount for patients paying in full without insurance; family bundle discount when 3+ family members are seen in the same visit.

---

## Payment Information

### Accepted Payment Methods

Cash, credit/debit card, HSA/FSA cards, and third-party payment plans (e.g., CareCredit).

### Payment Terms

Payment due at time of service unless a payment plan has been arranged in advance.

### Cancellation Policy

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may incur a $50 missed-appointment fee.

---

## Pricing Limitations

### Excluded Services

Orthodontics (braces/Invisalign) are not currently offered in-house; the practice refers out for these cases.

### Custom Pricing Conditions

Implants and veneers are always case-dependent and can never be quoted without an in-person consultation.

---

## Customer Guidance

### When Pricing Can Be Shared

Standard preventive and cosmetic whitening pricing can be shared directly by the AI.

### When Human Approval Is Required

Any deviation from listed prices, or any payment-plan negotiation, must go to front-desk staff.

### When a Custom Quote Is Necessary

Implants, veneers, root canals, and any case involving multiple procedures.
