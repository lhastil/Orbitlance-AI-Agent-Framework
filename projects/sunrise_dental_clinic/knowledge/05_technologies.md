Each entry follows the Technology Template embedded in `core/knowledge/05_technologies.md` (no dedicated `core/templates/technologies.md` currently exists — see Notes below).

## Digital X-Ray Imaging

**Category:** Clinical Technology

**Purpose:** Captures dental X-rays digitally for faster, lower-radiation imaging.

**Use Cases:** Cavity detection, root canal evaluation, implant planning.

**Compatibility:** Used in all treatment rooms; images available to patients immediately after capture.

---

## Practice Management Software

**Category:** Operations Platform

**Purpose:** Manages patient records, scheduling, billing, and insurance claims in one system.

**Use Cases:** Appointment booking, patient chart access, insurance claim submission.

**Compatibility:** Integrated with the online booking widget and patient reminder system.

---

## Online Booking Widget

**Category:** Patient-Facing Tool

**Purpose:** Lets patients view availability and book appointments directly from the website.

**Use Cases:** New patient booking, routine cleaning scheduling, appointment rescheduling.

---

## Patient Communication & Reminder System

**Category:** Automation Platform

**Purpose:** Sends automated appointment reminders and follow-up messages via text and email.

**Use Cases:** Appointment reminders, post-treatment follow-up, recall notices for overdue cleanings.

---

## AI Receptionist (Orbitlance Framework)

**Category:** AI Automation

**Purpose:** Answers common patient questions, assists with scheduling, and handles after-hours inquiries, following the Healthcare Industry Playbook's boundaries.

**Use Cases:** Answering FAQ, guiding emergency callers, collecting new-patient information before a human follow-up.

**Limitations:** Never diagnoses conditions, recommends treatment, or replaces the practice's clinical staff — per `core/guardrails/safety.md` and the Healthcare Industry Playbook.
