# FAQ

## Purpose

Defines the most frequently asked questions and their approved answers.

This document enables AI Agents to provide fast, accurate, and consistent responses to common customer inquiries.

---

## Responsibilities

- Answer common questions
- Reduce repetitive conversations
- Provide consistent information
- Improve customer experience
- Support self-service

---

## Must Include

- Frequently asked questions
- Approved answers
- Clear and concise responses
- Relevant follow-up actions (if applicable)

---

## Must Not Include

- Pricing negotiations
- Internal company policies
- Confidential information
- Unverified answers
- Temporary announcements

## Notes

Answers should be concise, accurate, and easy to understand.

If a question cannot be fully answered using this document, the AI should follow the Fallback Responses module or escalate when appropriate.

The FAQ should be reviewed regularly based on real customer conversations and updated as new recurring questions emerge.
