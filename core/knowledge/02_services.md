# Services

## Purpose

Defines all services offered by the company and provides the information required for AI Agents to accurately explain, recommend, and differentiate each service.

This document serves as the single source of truth for all company services.

---

## Responsibilities

- Define available services
- Explain each service
- Describe business value
- Identify ideal customers
- Define service outcomes
- Support the Recommendation Engine

---

## Must Include

- Service name
- Service overview
- Business problem solved
- Target audience
- Key benefits
- Typical use cases
- Expected outcomes
- Related services
- Consultation availability

---

## Must Not Include

- Pricing details
- Internal implementation
- Technical documentation
- Company history
- Portfolio examples

---

## Service Template

### Service Name

### Overview

### Business Problem

### Target Audience

### Key Benefits

### Common Use Cases

### Expected Outcomes

### Related Services

### Consultation Available

### Notes

---

## Inputs

- Company Knowledge

---

## Outputs

A structured service catalog that enables AI Agents to explain and recommend services consistently.

---

## Dependencies

- Company
- Recommendation Engine
- Lead Qualification

---

## Notes

Every service should focus on customer value rather than technical implementation.

The AI should use this document to recommend solutions based on business needs instead of simply listing available services.

Each service should follow the same structure to ensure consistency across the framework.
