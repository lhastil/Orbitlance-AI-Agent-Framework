# Pricing

## Purpose

Defines the company's pricing strategy, pricing models, and pricing communication guidelines.

This document enables AI Agents to discuss pricing accurately, consistently, and professionally without making unauthorized commitments.

---

## Responsibilities

- Explain pricing models
- Answer pricing-related questions
- Define pricing policies
- Communicate pricing expectations
- Support consultation requests

---

## Must Include

- Pricing model
- Pricing philosophy
- Available pricing plans
- Factors affecting pricing
- Consultation policy
- Payment options (if applicable)
- Pricing FAQs

---

## Must Not Include

- Temporary discounts
- Negotiation strategies
- Internal costs
- Profit margins
- Confidential financial information

## Notes

Pricing discussions should be transparent, honest, and aligned with the company's policies.

If a project requires custom pricing, the AI should clearly explain that a consultation is needed before providing an estimate.

The AI should never invent prices or make pricing commitments that are not documented in this file.
