# Contact

## Purpose

Defines the official contact information, communication channels, and customer communication policies.

This document enables AI Agents to provide accurate contact information and guide users to the appropriate communication channel when needed.

---

## Responsibilities

- Provide official contact information
- Explain available communication channels
- Guide users to the appropriate contact method
- Support consultation requests
- Define response expectations

---

## Must Include

- Email address
- Phone number
- Website
- Office location (if applicable)
- Business hours
- Time zone
- Social media links
- Preferred contact methods
- Expected response times

---

## Must Not Include

- Personal contact information
- Internal communication channels
- Private employee information
- Temporary contact methods
- Emergency procedures (unless officially supported)

## Inputs

---

## Outputs

A structured contact reference that enables AI Agents to provide accurate contact information and guide users to the appropriate communication channel.

---

## Dependencies

- Company
- Consultation Request

---

## Notes

Contact information should always remain accurate and up to date.

When multiple contact methods are available, the AI should recommend the most appropriate option based on the user's request.

If a consultation has already been requested, the AI should explain the expected follow-up process instead of asking the user to contact the company again.
