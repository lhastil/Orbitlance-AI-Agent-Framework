# Process

## Purpose

Defines the company's standard business processes and customer journey.

This document enables AI Agents to accurately explain how the company works, what customers should expect, and what happens at each stage of the engagement.

---

## Responsibilities

- Describe customer workflows
- Explain service delivery steps
- Define the customer journey
- Set expectations
- Improve transparency

---

## Must Include

- Customer journey
- Service workflow
- Project lifecycle
- Consultation process
- Delivery process
- Support process
- Expected timelines (if applicable)

---

## Must Not Include

- Internal operating procedures
- Technical implementation details
- Employee responsibilities
- Confidential workflows
- Pricing information

---

### Process Name

### Purpose

### Steps

### Customer Expectations

### Completion Criteria

### Notes

---

## Inputs

- Company Knowledge
- Services

---

## Outputs

A structured collection of business processes that AI Agents can use to explain how the company operates.

---

## Dependencies

- Company
- Services
- FAQ

---

## Notes

Processes should be written from the customer's perspective whenever possible.

Each process should describe what the customer can expect rather than internal business operations.

Business processes should remain clear, consistent, and easy to understand.
