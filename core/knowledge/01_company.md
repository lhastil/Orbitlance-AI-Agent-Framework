# Company

## Purpose

Defines the company's identity, mission, business background, and core information used by AI Agents.

This document serves as the primary source of truth about the company.

---

## Responsibilities

- Describe the company
- Define the company's mission
- Define the company's vision
- Explain the company's values
- Define target customers
- Describe the company's expertise

---

## Must Include

- Company name
- Company overview
- Mission
- Vision
- Core values
- Industries served
- Target audience
- Business goals
- Geographic availability
- Supported languages

---

## Must Not Include

- Detailed service descriptions
- Pricing information
- FAQs
- Portfolio
- Technical implementation details

## Notes

This file should remain business-focused and independent of any specific AI implementation.

Other knowledge files should reference this document rather than duplicate company information.
