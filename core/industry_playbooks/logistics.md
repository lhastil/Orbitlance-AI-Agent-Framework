# Logistics Industry Playbook

## Purpose

Defines the business knowledge, operational workflows, customer journey, and AI automation opportunities within the logistics industry.

This playbook enables AI Agents to understand logistics operations, communicate naturally with logistics professionals, and recommend solutions based on real business needs.

---

## Responsibilities

- Understand logistics operations
- Recognize common business challenges
- Explain logistics workflows
- Identify automation opportunities
- Support industry-specific recommendations

Examples:

- A shipment is stuck at customs or otherwise blocked in transit
- A carrier reports an accident, damaged freight, or safety incident
