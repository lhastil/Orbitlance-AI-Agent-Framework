# FAQ Template

## Purpose

Provides a standardized template for documenting frequently asked questions and their approved answers.

The objective is to ensure that AI Agents deliver accurate, consistent, and approved responses to common customer inquiries.

---

## Responsibilities

- Document common customer questions
- Define approved answers
- Reduce repetitive support requests
- Improve response consistency
- Support customer self-service
- Minimize misinformation

---

## Template Goal

Capture frequently asked questions and their official answers in a structured format that can be reused across AI Agents.

## Validation Checklist

Before publishing, verify that:

- The question is clearly written.
- The answer is accurate and approved.
- Related resources are referenced.
- Escalation conditions are defined when necessary.
- Similar questions are identified.

## Notes

Each frequently asked question should have its own completed template.

Approved answers should remain concise, accurate, and aligned with current business policies.

The information collected here becomes the primary source for the FAQ Knowledge file and should be reviewed regularly as products, services, or policies evolve.
