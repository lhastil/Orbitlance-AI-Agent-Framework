# Contact Template

## Purpose

Provides a standardized template for documenting all official business contact information and communication channels.

The objective is to ensure that AI Agents always provide accurate, consistent, and up-to-date contact information while guiding customers to the appropriate communication channel.

---

## Responsibilities

- Document contact information
- Define communication channels
- Identify department contacts
- Specify availability
- Support customer communication
- Improve response consistency

---

## Template Goal

Capture all customer-facing contact information required for AI Agents to connect customers with the appropriate business representative or communication channel.
