# Company Template

## Purpose

Provides a standardized template for collecting and organizing essential company information.

The objective is to establish a complete business profile that serves as the primary knowledge source for all AI Agents built using the Orbitlance AI Agent Framework.

---

## Responsibilities

- Define the company's identity
- Describe the business
- Document core business information
- Establish communication style
- Provide organizational context
- Support knowledge consistency

---

## Template Goal

Capture all fundamental company information required for an AI Agent to understand the business it represents.
