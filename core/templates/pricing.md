# Pricing Template

## Purpose

Provides a standardized template for documenting pricing models, packages, billing methods, and pricing policies.

The objective is to ensure that AI Agents provide accurate, consistent, and approved pricing information while respecting business rules and pricing limitations.

---

## Responsibilities

- Document pricing models
- Define service packages
- Describe billing methods
- Explain pricing policies
- Identify pricing limitations
- Support accurate pricing communication

---

## Template Goal

Capture all pricing-related information required for AI Agents to explain costs, pricing structures, and purchasing options accurately.
