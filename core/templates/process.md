# Process Template

## Purpose

Provides a standardized template for documenting business processes and operational workflows.

The objective is to ensure that AI Agents understand how the business operates, allowing them to accurately guide customers through each stage of the service lifecycle.

---

## Responsibilities

- Document business processes
- Define operational workflows
- Identify customer interactions
- Clarify business responsibilities
- Improve process consistency
- Support customer guidance

---

## Template Goal

Capture the complete lifecycle of a business process in a structured format that AI Agents can understand and explain consistently.

## Notes

Each business process should have its own completed template.

Process documentation should focus on how the business operates rather than implementation details.

The information collected here becomes the primary source for the Process Knowledge file and helps AI Agents guide customers through business operations consistently.
