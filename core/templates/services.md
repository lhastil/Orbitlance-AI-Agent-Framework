# Services Template

## Purpose

Provides a standardized template for documenting the products and services offered by a business.

The objective is to ensure that every AI Agent has complete, consistent, and structured knowledge about the company's offerings.

---

## Responsibilities

- Document available services
- Define service details
- Describe customer value
- Identify target customers
- Establish service limitations
- Support accurate recommendations

---

## Template Goal

Capture all information required for an AI Agent to understand, explain, compare, and recommend business services.
