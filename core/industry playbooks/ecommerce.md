# E-commerce Industry Playbook

## Purpose

Defines the business knowledge, operational workflows, customer journey, and AI automation opportunities within the e-commerce industry.

This playbook enables AI Agents to understand online retail operations, communicate naturally with e-commerce businesses and customers, and recommend AI solutions that improve sales, customer support, and operational efficiency.

---

## Responsibilities

- Understand e-commerce operations
- Recognize customer purchasing journeys
- Identify operational challenges
- Recommend AI automation opportunities
- Support e-commerce-specific conversations
