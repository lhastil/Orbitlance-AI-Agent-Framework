# Restaurant Industry Playbook

## Purpose

Defines the business knowledge, operational workflows, customer journey, and AI automation opportunities within the restaurant industry.

This playbook enables AI Agents to understand restaurant operations, communicate naturally with restaurant staff and customers, and recommend AI solutions based on real restaurant needs.

---

## Responsibilities

- Understand restaurant operations
- Recognize customer ordering workflows
- Identify operational challenges
- Recommend AI automation opportunities
- Support restaurant-specific conversations

## Escalation Considerations

Industry-specific urgent scenarios that should trigger escalation to a human, supplementing (never replacing) the universal conditions in `core/guardrails/escalation.md`.

Examples:

- A customer reports a suspected allergic reaction or foodborne illness
- A significant order dispute involving a refund beyond standard policy
- A safety incident on the premises
