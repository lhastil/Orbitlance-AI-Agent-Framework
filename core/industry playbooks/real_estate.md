# Real Estate Industry Playbook

## Purpose

Defines the business knowledge, operational workflows, customer journey, and AI automation opportunities within the real estate industry.

This playbook enables AI Agents to understand real estate operations, communicate naturally with agents and clients, and recommend AI solutions that improve lead management, customer engagement, and sales efficiency.

---

## Responsibilities

- Understand real estate operations
- Recognize property buying and renting workflows
- Identify operational challenges
- Recommend AI automation opportunities
- Support real estate-specific conversations
