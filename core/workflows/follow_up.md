# Follow-up Workflow

## Purpose

Defines the standard process for following up with qualified leads after an interaction.

The objective is to maintain engagement, provide timely communication, and maximize conversion opportunities without creating unnecessary pressure.

---

## Workflow Goal

Ensure that every qualified lead receives the appropriate follow-up based on their conversation outcome and business status.

---

## Trigger

Start this workflow when:

- A consultation request has been submitted.
- A qualified lead requires follow-up.
- A customer requested additional time before making a decision.
- The business team schedules a follow-up action.

---

## Prerequisites

The AI should have access to:

- Customer information
- CRM status
- Conversation summary
- Consultation status
- Previous follow-up history

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1 — Review Customer Context

Understand:

- Business type
- Requested service
- Conversation outcome
- Current lead status
- Previous interactions

---

### Step 2 — Determine Follow-up Objective

Examples:

- Confirm consultation request
- Answer remaining questions
- Request missing information
- Re-engage an inactive lead
- Provide updates from the business team

---

### Step 3 — Choose the Appropriate Communication

Possible channels:

- Email
- Phone call
- SMS
- AI Chat
- CRM Task

The communication method should follow the client's preferred workflow.

---

### Step 4 — Deliver the Follow-up

The communication should be:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Brief
- Helpful
- Relevant

Avoid repetitive or unnecessary messages.

---

### Step 5 — Record the Outcome

Update the CRM with:

- Follow-up completed
- Customer response
- New requirements
- Updated lead status
- Next action

---

## Follow-up Principles

Every follow-up should be:

- Timely
- Helpful
- Personalized
- Respectful
- Business-focused

---

## Must Include

- Customer context
- Purpose of the follow-up
- Clear next step
- CRM update after completion

---

## Must Not Include

- Repeated unsolicited messages
- Pressure to purchase
- Unsupported promises
- Irrelevant information

---

## Decision Points

If the customer responds:

➡ Continue the appropriate workflow.

If additional discovery is required:

➡ Return to Discovery Workflow.

If the customer requests a consultation:

➡ Continue to Consultation Workflow.

If no response is received:

➡ Follow the business's follow-up policy.

---

## Inputs

- CRM Record
- Conversation Summary
- Consultation Status

---

## Outputs

- Follow-up completed
- Updated CRM
- Updated lead status
- Next action

---

## Dependencies

- CRM Synchronization Workflow
- Consultation Workflow
- Company Knowledge

---

## Success Criteria

A follow-up is successful when:

- The customer receives relevant communication.
- The CRM is updated accurately.
- The next action is clearly defined.
- The customer relationship is strengthened.

---

## Notes

Follow-ups should always create value for the customer.

The objective is to continue the relationship—not simply to send another message.
